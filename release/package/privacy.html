My Shortcuts privacy notice

Version 1.2.0

Stored on your device

Your shortcut links, background settings, and selected photo are saved in local extension storage. Photos are resized on your device. The extension does not upload photos or backups, run analytics, or send data to the developer.

Optional website icons

Website icons are off by default. Offline letter icons do not send your shortcut domains anywhere.

If you enable website icons and grant Firefox's optional browsing-activity permission, the extension requests icons from Google's favicon service. Each request contains the hostname of a displayed saved shortcut. Full shortcut URLs, paths, query parameters, searches, and photos are not included.

Google also receives normal connection information, which can include your IP address, browser/request information, and cookies allowed by your browser settings. Google's handling of these requests is governed by its privacy policy.

You can turn website icons off in the extension's edit controls, or revoke the optional permission in Firefox's add-on permissions. No new icon requests are made after permission is revoked. Google/browser caches can retain previously requested icons.

Your controls

Remove individual links in edit mode, reset the background, remove your photo, or export links to a text file you control. Uninstalling the add-on removes its extension storage; separately downloaded backup files remain where you saved them.

Opening a shortcut navigates to that website normally; that website's own privacy policy applies.

For questions, use the support or developer contact available on the My Shortcuts Mozilla Add-ons listing.
